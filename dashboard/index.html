What that is worth

Bookings become money via two figures the website cannot observe: your close rate and what a customer pays.

The voice agent

A parallel route to a booking rather than a funnel step — Ava can book someone who never opens the calendar, so it is counted apart.

On the LTV formula. PostHog derives lifetime value as ARPU ÷ churn. Novera sells a build once and a receptionist monthly, so the setup fee is added as a one-off: setup + (monthly ÷ churn). A churn of zero would make LTV infinite, so it is treated as unset.

Where each number comes from. The funnel and the voice figures are measured on the site and read live from PostHog. The commercial inputs are marked configured when they come from environment variables and live when a revenue query supplies them. Nothing is estimated on your behalf.
